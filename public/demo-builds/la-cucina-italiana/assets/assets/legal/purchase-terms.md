# Purchase Terms — La Cucina Italiana Premium

**Effective date:** August 30, 2026
**App:** La Cucina Italiana
**Developer:** G Thompson

## What Premium is
"Premium" is a single, **one-time, non-consumable** in-app purchase. There is
**no subscription**. Buying it once grants a **lifetime** entitlement to the
premium features for the purchasing account.

## What Premium unlocks
- Unlimited collections and recipe planning
- Full-resolution offline recipe photo pack
- Recipe and shopping-list PDF export

The base application remains fully usable without Premium: every recipe, Cook
Mode, search, the shopping list, saved recipes, personal notes and ratings,
backup and restore, and all settings and legal information are free and are
never locked to force payment.

## Price and payment
The price shown in the app is supplied by Google Play for your region and
currency, including applicable taxes. Payment is charged to your Google Play
account. The developer never receives your card or banking details.

## Restoring your purchase
Premium is tied to your store account. After reinstalling the app or switching
devices, use **Settings → Restore purchases** (or the Restore option on the
Premium screen) to re-activate it at no additional cost.

## Refunds
Refunds are handled by Google Play under its refund policy. The developer cannot
process store refunds directly.

## Pending, cancelled, refunded or revoked purchases
If a purchase is pending, the entitlement activates once the store confirms it.
If a purchase is cancelled, refunded, or revoked by the store, the entitlement
is removed.

## Changes
These Purchase Terms may be updated if the premium offering changes. Existing
lifetime entitlements are honoured.
