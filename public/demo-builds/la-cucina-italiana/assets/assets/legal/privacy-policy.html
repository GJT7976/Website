Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 30, 2026
App: La Cucina Italiana
Developer: G. Thompson

La Cucina Italiana ("the app", "we", "us", or "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the app.

Information We Collect

La Cucina Italiana is designed to work primarily as an offline recipe application. We do not require you to create an account and we do not intentionally collect, store on our own servers, sell, rent, or share personally identifiable information.

Recipe Favorites and App Preferences

Features such as saved recipes, favorites, language choices, display settings, or other preferences may be stored locally on your device. This information is used only to provide the app's functions and remains on your device unless a future feature clearly tells you otherwise.

Internet and Network Access

The core recipe content may be available without an internet connection. If the app includes links that open external websites, app stores, email applications, or other services, those external services may process information according to their own privacy policies.

Analytics, Advertising, and Tracking

The app does not use advertising trackers, behavioral advertising, or third-party analytics unless this policy is updated to clearly disclose such use.

Payments and Purchases

If optional paid features or in-app purchases are offered, payment processing is handled by the applicable app store or payment provider, such as Google Play. We do not receive or store your full credit-card or banking information.

Permissions

The app will only request device permissions that are needed for an enabled feature. You may control permissions through your device settings. The app does not use device permissions to collect unrelated personal information.

Children's Privacy

La Cucina Italiana is a general-audience recipe application and is not designed to collect personal information from children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Data Security

Because the app is designed to minimize collection of personal information, the amount of user data exposed to us is limited. Information stored locally on your device is also subject to the security of your device and operating system.

Data Retention and Deletion

Locally stored favorites and preferences generally remain on your device until you remove them, clear the app's storage, or uninstall the app. If future versions add online accounts or cloud storage, this Privacy Policy will be updated with applicable retention and deletion procedures.

Third-Party Services

Your device operating system, app store, or any external website you choose to open may collect information independently. Their handling of information is governed by their own terms and privacy policies.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the app changes or when legal or platform requirements change. The revised policy will show an updated effective date.

Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy may be directed to the developer through the contact information provided on the La Cucina Italiana app store listing.
